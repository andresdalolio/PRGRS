# PRGRS — Official Brand Identity

**Status:** Brand copy approved; visual system is exploratory  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Updated:** 2026-10-26

## 1. Official brand language (keep exact)

**Eyebrow / brand statement:**

> YOUR TRAINING. YOUR JOURNEY.

**Product name:**

# PRGRS

**Primary tagline:**

> Track the work. See the growth.

**Brand rationale:**

> The name is incomplete by design. Because progress never ends.

The spelling **PRGRS** is a stylized contraction of **PROGRESS**, chosen intentionally as a recognizable minimal wordmark. In spoken contexts, pronounce it “progress.” The unfinished spelling represents an ongoing journey, not a missing product feature or a promise of perfection.

These words are settled brand decisions. Do not silently rewrite them in design explorations or implementation prompts.

## 2. Brand story

PRGRS is for people who do the work and want to see how far they have come. Progress isn't a single destination: it is repeated sessions, incremental improvements, and consistency through imperfect days. Finishing a session with 90% of exercises logged is still meaningful work—PRGRS can preserve that fact without falsely calling it 100%.

The application combines a practical training plan, frictionless recording, and useful historical context. It should help users get back to training, not keep them busy inside an app.

## 3. Brand values

1. **Progress over perfection** — support partially completed workouts without shaming.
2. **Consistency over intensity** — show regular effort and durable patterns, not just record lifts.
3. **Your journey** — different training styles (strength, hypertrophy, running, conditioning, functional training) fit in one product.
4. **Simplicity** — logging should be faster than the workout itself.
5. **Evidence of growth** — reflect real saved sessions and useful history, not invented motivational numbers.
6. **Trust** — offline edits, access permissions and performance history must be reliable.

## 4. Voice and copy

- Clear, concise, encouraging, confident; not aggressive or guilt-driven.
- Prefer useful action labels: **Continue workout**, **Finish workout**, **View history**, **Add note**, **Sync pending**.
- A partial workout is not a failure. Consider copy like “Session finished · 90% of exercises completed.”
- Never equate incomplete exercise logs with personal failure.
- Brand lines belong on onboarding/authentication, splash/marketing materials, maybe a subtle dashboard header—not repeated in every exercise card.

## 5. Visual design direction (proposed, NOT finalized)

- **Look:** minimal, premium, performance-oriented, approachable; unmistakably a modern mobile fitness app.
- **Typography:** clean legible sans serif, bold/compact PRGRS wordmark, excellent readability of reps/sets/weights.
- **Palette to explore:** near-black/charcoal, off-white and neutral gray, with an electric-lime or restrained green accent. These are concepts, **not locked hex codes**. Consider accessible alternatives.
- **Light and dark:** equally polished; dark mode should not be the only first-class theme.
- **Component language:** intentional spacing, simple surfaces, subtle grouping, clear touch-friendly check controls, restrained motion and decoration.
- **Workout priority:** prescriptions and actual logged results should be scannable in seconds. Show linked supersets/blocks as groups; don't overdecorate charts.
- **Accessibility:** test contrast, keyboard/focus states, screen reader labels and reduced-motion preferences.
- **Originality:** TrueCoach screenshots are functional inspiration; do not reproduce its proprietary UI pixel-for-pixel.

## 6. Where brand appears

- App title/PWA metadata: **PRGRS**.
- Splash/login/onboarding: possible use of “YOUR TRAINING. YOUR JOURNEY.” and “Track the work. See the growth.”
- About/brand explanation: “The name is incomplete by design. Because progress never ends.”
- Home: emphasize current training and progress first, not slogans.
- Coach area: same brand, more utilitarian layout.
- PWA icon: create an original, legible symbol/monogram suitable for small home-screen sizes; icon design is an open task.

## 7. Product design guardrails

- A workout can be finished at less than 100% exercise completion. Product copy and UI must preserve this distinction.
- Show informative progress trends; don't turn the product into an overly gamified streak-pressure tool.
- Short, efficient mobile flows are more important than elaborate animation.
- Support different training types without visually favoring bodybuilding or running exclusively.

## 8. Open decisions

The brand name, statement, tagline and rationale are **finalized**. Logo, icon, typeface, accent hue, exact color tokens and component design system are **not yet finalized**. Codex/design exploration may propose a cohesive first version without requiring endless micro-approvals.

## 9. How to use this file

When generating PRGRS implementation prompts, carry over the four exact brand lines and the user-experience principles. Give the implementation agent freedom to craft a good original design while respecting mobile usability, light/dark themes and accessibility.
